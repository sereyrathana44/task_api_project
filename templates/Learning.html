How it works

Read the short lesson, then answer four questions. You have 3 lives: a wrong answer costs one. Pass a level with at least 3 correct answers to unlock the next. Each correct answer earns 10 XP, and a perfect run adds 10 bonus XP.

Wrong question? Use "Report a problem" during a test.
